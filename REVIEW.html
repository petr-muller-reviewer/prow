What this PR does

  • Introduces InvalidCommitMsg / InvalidCommitMsgCheck structs in plugins.Configuration, allowing per-check enable/disable control via plugins.yaml.
  • Implements InvalidCommitMsgFor(org, repo) with two-pass repo-then-org precedence lookup, consistent with LgtmFor, ApproveFor, TriggerFor.
  • Replaces os.Getenv("ENABLE_FIXUP_CHECK") with config-driven checkFixup and checkIssueClosing flags in handle().
  • Adds validateInvalidCommitMsg() with check-name allowlisting and wires it into Configuration.Validate().
  • Both fixupPrefix and issueClosingKeywords checks are now independently configurable per org/repo.
