Dependency analysis

  • Freshness: v1.82.1 released 2026-07-15 (real grpc-go tag, not a pseudo-version) — ~34 days old at review time. Past the soak window, fine.

  • Changelog (1.79.3→1.82.1): xDS/RBAC bug fixes, a balancer-registry case-sensitivity behavior change, and a security fix in 1.82.1 (server-side mitigation for HTTP/2 frame-flooding DoS, plus xDS/RBAC ACL matcher fail-open fixes).
